Initiative Bot

How Signups Work

React to sign up

Each signup sheet has three custom emoji reactions, one per role:

🛡️ Tank ✨ Healer ⚔️ DPS
  • Reacting adds you to that role's list on the embed, in the order you signed up.
  • Reacting with a different role automatically removes your old reaction and moves you over — you can only hold one role at a time.
  • Removing your reaction removes you from the sheet entirely.
  • Signups stay open to everyone regardless of party size — role caps are only applied when parties are formed at close time.

Party formation

When a raid is closed, signups are bin-packed into parties by role, first-come-first-served, filling one party completely for a role before spilling into the next:

SizeTankHealerDPS
Small Party (4)112
Full Party (8)224
Alliance Raid (24)6612

Half-empty final parties are fine — a new party only opens once the previous one is full for that role. The formed parties are posted either to the configured party-announcements channel, or as a reply if none is set.

Timezone-aware scheduling

Raid times are entered as a date, time, and the organizer's own timezone. The bot converts that to a single point in time and renders it with Discord's native timestamp formatting, so every member sees the raid time and countdown in their own local time automatically — no manual conversion needed. Timezones are fixed UTC offsets rather than DST-aware, so it's worth double-checking times that straddle a DST switchover.

Slash Commands

/raid

Raid event signups

create Post a new raid signup sheet
OptionDetails
titleRequiredName of the raid event
sizeRequiredSmall Party (4), Full Party (8), or Alliance Raid (24)
dateOptionalDate, as DD-MM-YYYY
timeOptionalTime, 24h as HH:MM
timezoneOptionalYour timezone, so the time shows correctly for everyone else
imageOptionalBanner image for the signup sheet

If you set a time, date, time, and timezone must all be given together.

edit Update a raid's title, time, size, or image
OptionDetails
idRequiredRaid ID, shown in the footer of the signup embed
titleOptionalNew title
sizeOptionalNew party size
date / time / timezoneOptionalNew raid time — give all three together to change it
imageOptionalNew banner image

Leave an option blank to keep it unchanged — only what you fill in gets updated.

close Close signups and form parties from who signed up
OptionDetails
idRequiredRaid ID, shown in the footer of the signup embed
